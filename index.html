About

I am a fourth-year PhD student in the Computer Science Department at Rutgers University, where I am part of the CS Theory Group and advised by Karthik C. S. My current research is focused mainly on the theory of error-correcting codes.

Before coming to Rutgers, I was an undergraduate student in the Computer Science & Engineering Department at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology.
